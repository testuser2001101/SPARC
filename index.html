WHO WE ARE

Purposeful learning, people-centred support.

SPARC – Counselling, Training & Support is a professional initiative focused on training, counselling, capacity building, workshops, seminars and personal development services.

Led by Sushma Pandey, SPARC draws on more than 13 years of professional experience across government and NGO settings, including programme coordination, social welfare, stakeholder engagement, training and counselling-related work.

Our approach is practical, participatory and responsive to the needs of organisations, teams and individuals.
